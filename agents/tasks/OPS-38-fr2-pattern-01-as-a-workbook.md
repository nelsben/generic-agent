## Why

Feature revision 2 (FR2), Ben's chapter notes on pattern 01. He wants the page to explain the philosophy of route, gather, reduce: how Agentforce can shape customer context without Data Cloud, in two parts, "the business impact and then ... the architecture". It should show that "Everyone wants deal history, service history, and an insight into the customer", how easy it is, and how the narrative over the summary changes by industry. It should cover how it scales, "how ... to build a graph of all of the data, pass it to an LLM, and make sure that the summary is accurate", focused model calls as the design principle, "truly beautiful sequence diagrams" with auto number and colour, the reduction structure with other histories (case history, order history), why you can trust it, and limits as cards. Covers ledger rows F44 to F53 in docs/plans/feature-revision-2.md.
